A NOTE FROM THE AUTHOR

About Me

✧ ━━━━━━━━━ ✧

Hi! I'm Poorvaja S (23MIC0030), and this is my portfolio — a little space built around the things I love, the stories that fascinate me, and the interests that shape the way I see the world.

I'm someone who loves stories in every form imaginable. Books, mangas, anime, TV shows, and pretty much anything that has a story to tell — I'm interested in all of it. There's something about stories that excites me, whether it's getting lost in a fictional world, understanding a character, or discovering the little details that make a story unforgettable. I don't just enjoy stories; I'm fascinated by them.

Another passion of mine is history. To me, history is everything. It's who we were, how we got here, what we learned, and everything we can still learn from the past. Every little detail holds a story worth discovering, and I believe preserving history is just as important as understanding it. There's so much of our world hidden in the things we've forgotten, overlooked, or left behind, and I want to be part of preserving those stories.

Recently, I've discovered a new interest in Natural Language Processing (NLP), and honestly, it feels like a place where all my interests can come together. The idea that we can use technology to understand language, uncover patterns in text, explore stories, and preserve the knowledge hidden within words fascinates me. It connects my love for stories and history with my interest in computing in a way I hadn't quite imagined before.

This portfolio is a reflection of that curiosity. It's about exploring the connections between language, stories, history, and technology — and discovering what we can learn when we look a little closer.

Because, at the end of the day, I'm interested in stories. The ones people tell, the ones history leaves behind, and the ones we can uncover through the words we use.

— Poorvaja ✦
